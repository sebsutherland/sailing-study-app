Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the sailing study app (“the app”) collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is run by Sebastian Sutherland, an individual, who is responsible for your information. Questions or requests go to seb.sutherland@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You need an account, and the account needs an email address.
  • The app saves your study progress and anything you write in your logbook, so it is there on every device you sign in on.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your information is not sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app at any time.
  • The app is for people 13 and older.

2. What the app collects

Your account

  • Your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. We never see it.

Your study history, so your progress follows you between devices:

  • Which lessons you have started or finished, and your scores.
  • Your answers to quiz questions, including what you typed into fill-in answers, and whether each was right.
  • Your results on practice drills, sample tests and scenarios, with the time each took.

Your logbook, if you use it:

  • The date you sailed, the boat, the conditions, the skills you practised, and any notes you write. These are free text, so they contain whatever you choose to type. Please don't put other people's personal details in them.

What the app does not collect: your name (unless you type it into a note), your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any analytics about how you use the app.

3. How it is used

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to save your progress and logbook, to keep them in step across your devices, and to work out things like which questions to review next. It isn't used for advertising, for profiling, or for anything else.

4. Where it is kept, and who else handles it

  • On your device. A copy is stored in the app so it works without a signal.
  • Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, stores your account and synced data on servers in the United States. Supabase also sends the account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password). It acts on our instructions and may not use your data for its own purposes.
  • Apple delivers the app (App Store / TestFlight) under its own privacy policy. The app does not send your study data to Apple.

Your data is sent to and from Supabase over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Database rules stop any account from reading or changing another account's data.

Nothing else receives your information. We will disclose it only if the law requires it.

5. How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and all the study history and logbook entries attached to it are deleted from the database straight away. Copies in our provider's routine backups expire on their normal cycle and are not restored.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account removes everything immediately. You can also email us and we will do it.
  • See or correct: email us and we will send you a copy of your data, or correct it.
  • Logbook entries can be edited or deleted in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), the law may give you further rights, such as objecting to how your data is used or complaining to a data-protection authority. Email us to use any of them; we will reply within 30 days. We don't sell or "share" personal information as the California law defines those terms.

7. Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. If a change affects how your existing data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
